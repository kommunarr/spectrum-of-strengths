Heritage · systems · visionary leadership

Foundations

This page sets out the founding direction. It can grow as contributors, evidence, and historical records are confirmed.

Origins

The starting point is a belief that heritage, history, and tradition hold knowledge that can help shape better systems today. The archive will document sources and stories as they are gathered and approved for publication.

Purpose

We aim to celebrate strengths, identify unmet social needs and institutional gaps, and explore products and practical changes that meet needs, create shared value, and benefit neurodivergent communities, including autistic and ADHD people, and the wider systems around them.

Contributors

Contributor and partner profiles will be added when names, roles, and descriptions are confirmed and approved for sharing.

Building relationships

We are focusing first on the relationships and due process needed to establish this work in Canada. We intend to take the time needed to build trust with national bodies and professionals before describing joint work publicly. Cross-border work remains a later intention. The journal will distinguish conversations and plans from confirmed commitments, with dates and source context when available.

Embodiment

We put the purpose into practice by connecting evidence and lived experience with ways to create, transform, preserve, and fairly share value.
