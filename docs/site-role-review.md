# Two-site role review packet

Editorial draft, 2026-10-16. This file is in a public repository. It contains
only observations from public pages and proposed wording; it does not reproduce
private correspondence or approve new public claims.

## What the public pages currently say

| Page | Observable description | Verification limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bilingual public-record site](https://kommunarr.github.io/spectrum-of-strengths/) | English and French routes introduce Spectrum of Strengths, its four value ideas, Foundations, and an archive with one dated opening entry. Home describes a developing public record and future responses. Events and Contact are marked in development. | The opening entry is dated 2026-09-27. Its description of non-operating programs and community centres should be read in that publication context, not silently revised as if it were written today. |
| [Foundation site](https://spectrumofstrengthsfoundation.com/) | The English-language homepage presents Spectrum of Strengths Foundation, its mission and architecture, an active Living Lab, and statements of recognition and relationships. It has a contact email link. | These are statements on that website. This review has not independently verified their scope, supporting records, or how they relate to the bilingual site's earlier wording. |

Neither homepage currently explains the relationship between the two sites or
links to the other. The names are similar enough that a visitor may reasonably
assume they represent one organization or successive versions of one site. A
short, approved explanation would prevent that ambiguity without rewriting the
dated opening record.

## Proposed public wording for focused review

Place one small note near the bilingual site's introduction and a direct link
to the Foundation site. This text is a draft, not approved site copy.

**English**

> This bilingual site keeps a public record of Spectrum of Strengths as its
> story develops. For information about the Foundation's current work, visit
> the Spectrum of Strengths Foundation website (in English).

**Français**

> Ce site bilingue tient un dossier public sur Spectrum of Strengths au fil de
> son évolution. Pour connaître les activités actuelles de la Fondation,
> consultez le site de Spectrum of Strengths Foundation (en anglais).

Keep the existing opening entry and its original publication date. If the
organization's status has changed since then, add a new dated update and adjust
present-tense introductory copy after the facts are confirmed. Do not transfer
institutional recognition, partner names, or measured outcomes from the other
site into this archive solely because they appear there.

## Three short questions for the creator

1. Is it accurate to describe this bilingual site as the community's public
   record and the Foundation site as the place for current organizational
   information? May the two sites link to each other using those descriptions?
2. Has anything changed since the 2026-09-27 opening entry that this site's
   present-tense description should reflect? In particular, what work is
   operating now, and what remains a plan? A rough correction is enough.
3. What one earlier piece of community work may we record first? A rough note
   or public link is enough; please say if any names or details must stay
   private.

The maintainer can turn the answers into concise English and French copy and
ask for review only of specific facts and permissions. If the first answer is
no, keep the current site unchanged while finding the correct relationship
wording. If the second answer is uncertain, retain the dated opening entry and
avoid adding a new current-state claim. If the third answer is not ready, the
archive can continue with its approved opening entry.
