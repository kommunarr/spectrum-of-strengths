## Verified source facts

- The [Public Health Agency of Canada's English strategy](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/publications/diseases-conditions/canada-autism-strategy.html)
  and [French strategy](https://www.canada.ca/fr/sante-publique/services/publications/maladies-et-affections/strategie-autisme-canada.html)
  each give 2024-09-26 as their publication date.
- The agency says the strategy and [Framework for Autism in
  Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/services/diseases/autism-spectrum-disorder-asd/what-canada-doing.html)
  were released on that date. It describes the strategy as a multi-year plan
  to support implementation of the framework.
- The strategy names five priority areas: screening, diagnosis, and services;
  economic inclusion; data collection, public health surveillance, and
  research; public awareness, understanding, and acceptance; and tools and
  resources. It describes engagement with Autistic people and others during
  development, including national conferences in 2022 and 2024.

These sources describe autism policy. They do not by themselves establish
claims about ADHD, the organization's products, its relationships, or the
strategy's results. The source publication date is not the site's future
publication date.

### English title

Canada's 2024 Autism Strategy as a systems record

### English summary

On September 26, 2024, the Public Health Agency of Canada published Canada's
Autism Strategy alongside the Framework for Autism in Canada. The strategy
sets out federal priorities for services, economic inclusion, research,
public understanding, and access to tools and resources. It also records
consultation with Autistic people and others during its development. This
public document provides a dated point of reference for examining how
Canadian systems describe needs and proposed responses.

### Titre français

La Stratégie pour l'autisme au Canada de 2024 : un repère sur les systèmes

### Résumé français

Le 26 septembre 2024, l'Agence de la santé publique du Canada a publié la
Stratégie pour l'autisme au Canada en même temps que le Cadre pour l'autisme
au Canada. La stratégie présente des priorités fédérales concernant les
services, l'inclusion économique, la recherche, la compréhension du public
et l'accès à des outils et à des ressources. Elle rend aussi compte de
consultations menées auprès de personnes autistes et d'autres participantes
et participants pendant son élaboration. Ce document public fournit un
repère daté pour examiner la façon dont les systèmes canadiens décrivent
les besoins et les réponses envisagées.
