Accessibility standards

The site uses clear headings, keyboard navigation, a skip link, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.

Motion on the Home and archive pages

The value ideas on Home and the archive themes each change automatically about every 6 seconds with a gentle fade. Rotation pauses while your pointer is over a carousel and resumes when it leaves. Keyboard focus stops rotation until you use the play control. Use the small controls to pause, play, or move between ideas. If your device is set to reduce motion, each carousel is replaced by a static layout showing all its ideas.

Review and feedback

Automated accessibility checks and the organization's manual review have been completed. The organization reported no actionable high or medium findings. Accessibility feedback cannot yet be submitted through this website; contact options will be added when they are ready.
