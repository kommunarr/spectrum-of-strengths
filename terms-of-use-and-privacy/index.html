Privacy & site information

This page summarizes how the website currently works. It will be updated before new ways of submitting information or other data practices are introduced.

Information submitted through this site

The current site does not provide working contact, newsletter, event registration, or payment forms. It does not ask visitors to submit those kinds of personal information.

External websites

Some links may lead to external websites. Those sites have their own privacy and content practices.

Languages and content

Core site content is offered in English and French. External resources may be available only in the language used by their publishers.

Hosting and technical information
