Heritage · systems · visionary leadership

Key terms

These working definitions explain terms that have a particular meaning in our approach. They can be refined as the framework develops.

Heritage
Knowledge, histories, practices, and traditions carried forward by people and communities.
Visionary leadership
Setting a direction that looks ahead, recognizes needs, and brings people and resources together to make change possible.
Systems as a strategic anchor
The structures, relationships, and practices that shape how support and opportunity reach people.
Gap finding
Identifying where people’s needs are not being met, using evidence and lived experience to understand what is missing.
Gap bridging
Connecting an identified need to a practical response, service, product, or change in a system.
Value capture
Making sure that benefits created through knowledge, work, and relationships are recognized and shared fairly.
Value transformation
Changing existing resources, practices, or services so that they provide greater benefit.
Value creation
Developing something useful in response to a need, such as an idea, tool, product, or service.
Value preservation
Keeping valuable knowledge, practices, and relationships available for future use.
Living lab
An ongoing way of learning from real experiences and evidence while ideas and responses are being developed.
