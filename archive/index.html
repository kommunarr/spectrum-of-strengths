Archive & journal

A living record with heritage and systems as its anchor, connecting histories, research, experiences, needs, ideas, and progress.

What the record will include

Heritage and systems

Historical records and traditions that help explain how systems have developed.

Research and evidence

Research and documented evidence related to social needs and possible responses.

Lived experience

Experiences and perspectives shared with permission and presented with care.

Gaps and responses

Examples of unmet needs, the systems around them, and ideas for products or changes that bridge the gaps and create shared value.

Plans and progress

Dated updates on plans, relationship building, work in progress, and confirmed milestones. Private correspondence can be cited without publishing the full message.

Published entries

Entries will celebrate strengths and show the path from evidence to action. When there is enough information to share, a documented need, proposed response, and potential shared value will appear together. Plans and correspondence will be dated without treating them as completed work.

  • Plans and progressConfirmedPublication date: September 27, 2026

    Spectrum of Strengths opens its public record

    Spectrum of Strengths is opening a public record rooted in heritage, history, tradition, dignity, and the strengths of neurodivergent communities, including autistic and ADHD people. We intend to bring research and lived experience together in an archive and living lab, identify unmet needs, and develop practical responses that create and share value. Our plans include products and future community centres. These are intentions under development; no community centres or programs are currently operating.
